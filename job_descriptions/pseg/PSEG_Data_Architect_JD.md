# Data Lake, Analytics, Optimization, and AI Architect
**Program:** PSEG Aligne V26 Upgrade and Implementation
**Client:** PSEG Energy Resources and Trade (ER&T)
**Platform:** FIS Aligne Version 26 (ETRM/CTRM)
**Engagement Type:** Time and Materials Consulting
**Duration:** September 2026 - May 2027

---

## Role Summary

Senior consulting architect responsible for the data lake strategy, analytics readiness, optimization roadmap, and AI governance framework on the PSEG Aligne V26 upgrade and reimplementation program. The engagement is architecture and planning, not production build. The primary output is a governed commercial data layer design, complete future project plan, and sequenced roadmap that PSEG can use to launch a follow-on data, analytics, optimization, and AI program with confidence.

---

## Responsibilities

### Data Architecture and Domain Design
1. Define priority Aligne commercial data domains for future data lake consumption, including trades, positions, prices, curves, schedules, nominations, allocations, settlements, invoices, counterparties, books, products, fees, charges, and accounting mappings.
2. Document source-to-target data movement logic at a conceptual level, including lineage, reconciliation rules, data quality rules, ownership, refresh cadence, access controls, and consumption patterns.
3. Design trusted data products aligned to downstream reporting, self-service analytics, optimization, and AI use cases.
4. Align the Aligne V26 implementation design to future data consumption so core integrations, reconciliation controls, and reporting patterns are not locked in ways that complicate the future data layer.

### Integration and Data Movement (Data Workstream)
5. Classify current interfaces as retain, remediate, replace, retire, or prepare for future governed data lake consumption.
6. Define reconciliation concepts connecting Aligne source data to curated data products, with exception handling and business ownership identified.
7. Collaborate with the AIF, integration, and technical workstreams to ensure data movement design supports future analytics and avoids duplication or fragmentation.

### Analytics, Optimization, and AI Readiness
8. Identify and prioritize analytics and optimization use cases, including P&L explanation, exposure monitoring, scheduling exceptions, invoice exceptions, trend analysis, settlement insights, and operational controls.
9. Create a governed AI readiness backlog using approved data lake and reporting data rather than uncontrolled direct access to Aligne production tables.
10. Define AI guardrails, prompt-safe data boundaries, and practical workflow candidates for AI-enabled commercial operations support.
11. Document governance needs, access controls, and consumption model for self-service analytics and future AI tooling.

### Future Program Planning
12. Deliver a practical future project plan for data lake implementation, including recommended sequencing, work packages, required roles, architecture decisions, infrastructure assumptions, data domain dependencies, and estimated delivery path.
13. Ensure the future roadmap is decision-ready, not conceptual - PSEG must be able to use it to initiate and fund a follow-on program without additional discovery.

### Governance and Delivery
14. Participate in biweekly architecture and design reviews with functional, technical, and PSEG architecture stakeholders during Stages 2 through 4.
15. Support Blueprint and Mobilization workstream planning, including confirming data domain ownership, tooling assumptions, and cloud platform standards with PSEG architecture.
16. Deliver the Data Lake Architecture and AI Readiness Assessment by end of Stage 2 (October 2026) and the full Future Data Lake, Analytics, Optimization, and AI Project Plan as a Stage 6 transition deliverable (May 2027).

## Constraints and Conditions

1. Production data lake build, production pipelines, dashboards, and optimization model implementation are explicitly out of scope. The role is architecture and planning only.
2. All AI and analytics work must use PSEG-approved data access patterns. Sensitive commercial data may not move into unauthorized tools or environments.
3. Data lake tooling, platform standards, and cloud patterns have not been committed by PSEG at proposal stage. These must be confirmed during Blueprint and Mobilization before architecture can be finalized.
4. PSEG Architecture, Data, and Security resources are allocated approximately 320 hours across the full engagement. The data architect must work within limited PSEG domain owner availability and plan engagement points early.
