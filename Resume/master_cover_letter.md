## Tone Guidelines

### Do
- Be confident but not arrogant
- Be warm and professional
- Be concise (no more than 5 paragraphs, under 200 words total)
- Use "I am excited to apply" or "I am excited to be considered"
- Reference experience generally that aligns with the role
- Show genuine interest in the company/mission when authentic
- Talk about what you've done and what you know generally
- Avoid naming specific prior organizations unless critical to the story

### Don't
- Use emojis or overly casual language
- Write more than 5 paragraphs or over 200 words
- Include generic statements without backing
- Use "Dear Hiring Manager" (use "Hi," or "Hi [First Name]," instead)
- Use em dashes (—)
- List out contact names/titles above the greeting
- Use justified paragraph alignment (always use left-aligned unless explicitly requested otherwise)

---

## Structure Summary

1. **Header**: Name, email, phone, LinkedIn, date
2. **Attention Line**: ATTN: [Role] Hiring Manager (or specific person title)
3. **Greeting**: Hi,
4. **Opening Paragraph**: Excitement + role + experience hook
5. **Body Paragraphs (1-2)**: Relevant experience, value proposition, company fit
6. **Closing Paragraph**: Thank you + forward-looking statement
7. **Sign-Off**: Sincerely, Mara Jorgensen
